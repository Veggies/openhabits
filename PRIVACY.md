# OpenHabits privacy policy

*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

OpenHabits doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts with us, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## Where your data is kept

Your habits, check-ins, notes and focus sessions are stored on your device. If you connect the app to a server, they're also stored on that server. The server is one that you or someone you trust runs, for example on a home NAS. We never see it or anything on it.

## The directory

If you turn on remote access on your server, it publishes its own public internet address to the OpenHabits directory, run by the developer at directory.ryanruddock.com. That lets your devices find your server when its address changes. The directory stores only the server's ID and its address. It doesn't store your name, your account, or any of your habits. Your devices look up that address only when they can't reach your server any other way.

The directory briefly uses the internet address a request comes from to stop abuse (rate limiting). It doesn't keep it.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Camera:** only to scan a pairing code shown on your other device. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
- **Local network:** to reach your server on your home network.
- **Notifications:** for the reminders you choose to set.

## Children

OpenHabits is suitable for everyone and doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions are welcome in the project's [Issues](https://github.com/Veggies/openhabits/issues).
